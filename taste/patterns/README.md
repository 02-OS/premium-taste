# Screen & Flow Patterns (`taste/patterns/`)

Directions (`quiet-luxury`, `cinematic-dark`, `precise-technical`, `editorial-print`) decide aesthetic register and first-screen tension. Brand references (`taste/references/`) show one structural idea on a real surface.

**Patterns decide product mechanics.** They govern how interactive screens behave across states (empty, loading, confirmation, sorting, multi-step abandonment, cancellation) regardless of surface styling.

Combine all three before writing UI code:

1. **Direction** (1 per screen) -> temperature, density, corner logic
2. **Reference** (1 primary structural pull) -> spatial thesis & what not to copy
3. **Pattern** (1 screen or flow recipe) -> exact states, keyboard/focus contracts, conversion or safety honesty
