# MUJI

**Tags:** `quiet-luxury` `retail` `label` `material` `no-signature-color` `plain-pack`

The goods, the packaging, and the way Ryohin Keikaku describes them. Not a measured stylesheet of one regional storefront. www.muji.com is the public entry; country sites differ.

## Description

Objects named for a material and a use. A polypropylene case, a cotton shirt, a notebook. Packaging is plain and uniform, and it carries a product fact and a price rather than a slogan or a designer credit. Stores put those objects in reach, in bulk, on unadorned fixtures. The company's own account, unchanged in substance since 1980, gives three manufacturing rules: choose the material, cut the process steps that do not change quality, and simplify the package.

## Key decisions

- The name of the thing is the name of what it is. Copy does not add a mood (`uniqueness.specific-copy`).
- There is no signature color to spend. The material, unbleached or as-made, is the color (`color.budget`).
- Packaging refuses ornament. A label that only restates the object is the whole graphic system.
- Designer credit stays off the object. The product is not a signed poster.
- "No brand" is a manufacturing rule. It is not an empty page (`density.not-empty`).

## Why it works

The decision a person is making is practical: which plain object, in which material, at which size. A campaign headline would compete with that decision (`hierarchy.one-job`). Because the package has nothing to advertise, the differences that matter (paper, cotton, a dimension, a price) can sit in one factual line and still be the hierarchy (`hierarchy.three-levels`).

## What to steal

- Name the object by material and function, then stop.
- One quiet ground. Let the material of the product be the variation between items.
- A label, not a badge: what it is, a size, a price.
- Removal with a reason. If a step does not change the object, it is not on the page either: no icon tile, no promotional ribbon, no second button.

## What not to copy

- A beige theme on the generic skeleton. Beige is not the lesson. The lesson is that the object states itself.
- Emptiness. A page with no object, no material, and no price is not MUJI. It is unfinished.
- An invented hex or an invented house font. This file has neither.
- Their full catalog grid under an unrelated hero. Steal the label.
- Treating "no logo" as a reason to remove the name of the product. The product name stays.

## Uniqueness

Hermès withholds a famous color from the chrome and spends it in the photograph. Aesop writes a short formula essay on warm paper. COS frames a garment on a person. MUJI has no signature color and almost no prose. The object is identified the way a bin in the shop is identified. Are.na is the tool version of a similar refusal, with the user's blocks in place of the goods.

## Product fit

Household goods, tools, a studio selling useful objects, a catalog of materials.

A poor fit for a film, a night listing, or a house whose entire point is one recognizable color. Also a poor fit for a dashboard.
