# MUBI

**Tags:** `cinematic-dark` `film` `bands` `stills` `square-corners` `one-action-color` `grotesk`

The public site at mubi.com, which behaves like a film journal that also sells a subscription. The player is a different surface and is not this note.

## Description

Full-bleed bands that alternate between a black room and a white page. Film stills occupy the black bands. Type is one grotesk, Riforma, including uppercase display lines at a regular-to-medium weight. Corners are square. Public captures in 2026 report a deep cobalt near `#001489` used for the action (a join button, an inline link) and a red near `#d71b1d` reserved for the print magazine, not sprinkled through the UI. The stills are saturated. The chrome is black, white, and that one blue.

## Key decisions

- Cinematic is a structure of bands and stills, not a dark theme.
- The page is willing to be white. Night is used when there is a picture that needs a room.
- One action color. Red is not a second accent for buttons; it belongs to the magazine.
- Uppercase is a display register for the journal's voice, on headlines, not on every label.
- Square corners. A rounded streaming tile would turn the journal into an app store.
- The still is the color. The interface does not grade the photograph with a glow.

## Why it works

The product is a point of view about films. The layout is a broadsheet: a picture, a line, a column (`uniqueness.one-idea`). Because black and white both exist, black stays meaningful (`color.dark-surfaces`). The cobalt is findable exactly because the rest of the chrome refuses hue (`color.accent-rarity`).

## What to steal

- Alternating rooms: a night band only where an image is the subject, a paper band where the text is the subject.
- One grotesk with a clear display register (case, size) and a quieter text register.
- Square tiles and square buttons.
- One deep, slightly strange action color, used a few times, not a rainbow of CTAs.
- A headline that states the product in plain words, set as type, not as gradient lettering.

## What not to copy

- Riforma, unless licensed. Any plain grotesk can take the role. Do not "upgrade" it to a fashion serif.
- Uppercase on every heading, button, and label (`type.all-caps-everything`). Keep caps for the display line.
- A black page with no still, plus a cobalt button. That is the skeleton in a dark coat.
- Their exact cobalt on a product that is not this journal.
- The streaming-library pattern of infinite poster shelves, which this note is not recommending. The lesson is the journal band.

## Uniqueness

Resident Advisor is a dense night listing with a red signal. Criterion is a library frame with numbers and essays. MUBI's public site is a broadsheet that changes rooms. Use it when the cinematic urge would otherwise become "make the SaaS page dark."

## Product fit

Film, photography, a journal, a premiere page, a subscription whose content is works.

A poor fit for settings, forms, and data tables. Those do not have stills, and a black band will not invent one.
