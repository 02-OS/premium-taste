# Premium Taste

This repository is a taste layer for coding agents. It is not a component library.

Before generating or restyling any interface, read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and follow its load order. The judgment rules are in [taste/principles.md](taste/principles.md). The checkable form is [taste/principles.yaml](taste/principles.yaml). After a first pass, follow [skills/critique-ui/SKILL.md](skills/critique-ui/SKILL.md).

Do not load the whole gallery. Pick one direction and one reference.
